The Process

How RPL Works

A straightforward four-stage process designed to assess your skills and experience against nationally recognised competency standards.

1

Free Eligibility Check

Speak with an RPL advisor who will assess your background, work history and experience to determine whether RPL is suitable for you — at no cost.

2

Evidence Collection

Your advisor guides you through the evidence collection process, helping you identify and compile the right documentation to support your RPL application.

3

Partner RTO Assessment

Your application is submitted to a partner Registered Training Organisation (RTO) who conducts the formal assessment against national competency standards.

4

Qualification Outcome

Upon successful assessment by the partner RTO, a nationally recognised qualification is issued. Your advisor will keep you informed throughout the process.

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the RPL process, eligibility and how Pathlyn works.

RPL is a formal assessment process that recognises the skills, knowledge and experience you have gained through work, life and previous study — and assesses them against the requirements of a national qualification. If you meet the required competency standards, you may be awarded a qualification or specific units without completing full formal training.
Eligibility varies depending on the qualification and the evidence you can provide. Generally, if you have been working in a relevant industry for at least 12 months, you may be a suitable candidate for RPL. The best way to find out is to complete a free eligibility check with one of our advisors, who will assess your background and advise on the most appropriate pathway.
The types of evidence that may support your RPL application include your resume, payslips, employment letters, professional references, work samples, photographs, videos, licences, certificates and any previous training records. Your advisor will guide you on the specific evidence requirements for your qualification and help you identify what you already have.
Timeframes vary depending on the qualification, the evidence available and the partner RTO assessment process. In many cases the process can be completed within 4 to 12 weeks. Your advisor will provide a realistic estimate during your eligibility check based on your individual circumstances.
No. Pathlyn is not a Registered Training Organisation. We are a specialist RPL consultancy and technology platform. We work with a network of partner RTOs who conduct the formal assessment and issue nationally recognised qualifications on your behalf. Qualifications are issued by the partner RTO, not by Pathlyn.
No. RPL is an assessment process and the outcome depends on the evidence you are able to provide and the assessment conducted by the partner RTO. Pathlyn does not offer guaranteed outcomes, issue qualifications directly, or use language such as "guaranteed qualification" or "instant certificate." We will always provide you with an honest assessment of your eligibility before you proceed.
Fees vary depending on the qualification and the partner RTO involved. The eligibility check is always free. During your initial consultation, your advisor will provide a transparent breakdown of all costs before you commit to anything. There are no hidden fees or surprises.
RPL is available for many nationally recognised qualifications across a wide range of industries. However, not every qualification or unit may be fully assessable through RPL. Some qualifications have mandatory practical components that must be completed through formal training. Our advisors will outline the most appropriate pathway based on your experience and the specific qualification you are pursuing.
